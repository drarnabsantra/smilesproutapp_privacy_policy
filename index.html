1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the SmileSprout Android application, developed by Arnab Santra ("we", "us", or "our").

SmileSprout is a family dental care companion offering:

  • Children Oral Care: a 2-minute brushing timer with music, child teething timelines, streak badges, and pediatric oral-care guides;
  • Pregnancy Oral Hygiene Care: trimester-based dental hygiene guidance, symptom and diet logs, and checklists;
  • Routine Reminders: notifications and alarms for morning and night brushing and dental visits;
  • Education: general oral health information. It is not a substitute for professional medical or dental advice.
Medical disclaimer: SmileSprout provides general information only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified dentist or doctor about your or your child's health.

2. Information We Collect

SmileSprout collects only what is needed to run its features. You can use many features in local guest mode without creating an account.

A. Information You Provide

  • Account details: if you create an account, your email address and either a password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it ourselves) or your Google account sign-in details, plus your display name.
  • Parent and child profiles: your name and your child's name or nickname and age, entered by the parent or guardian.
  • Health and wellness entries: pregnancy week and due date, self-reported dental symptoms, diet entries, dental visit appointments, oral-health assessments, and brushing session history and streaks.
  • Profile picture (optional): if you pick a photo from your gallery, only that image is uploaded to Firebase Storage. The app does not otherwise access your photos.
  • Preferences: language, reminder times, alarm sound, theme and font size.

B. Collected Automatically

  • Notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver reminders and dental tips to your device.
  • Technical information: Firebase services may automatically collect limited technical data, such as app installation identifiers and diagnostics, to keep the app working.

C. Stored on Your Device

Your logs and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. If you sync, a copy is also stored in your account in the cloud.

3. How We Use Information

  • Reminders and tracking: to schedule brushing alarms, show timers, streaks and history.
  • Cloud sync: to back up and restore your records across your devices using Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Notifications: to send reminders and dental tips you have enabled.
  • Account security: to sign you in and keep your data private to your account.

Our commitments:

  • We do not sell or rent personal or health information.
  • We do not use health logs or child profiles for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access your contacts, microphone, camera or device location. The "Find dentists" button simply opens Google Maps (or your browser) with a search; any location sharing happens inside Google Maps under your own settings.

4. Children's Privacy

  • For parents: SmileSprout is intended for use by parents, expectant mothers and legal guardians. It is not directed at children, and children are not expected to create accounts.
  • Child information: any child name or age is entered and managed only by the parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
  • No advertising: the app currently contains no advertising. If this changes, we will update this policy before ads are shown.
Parental rights: Parents and guardians can view, edit or delete any child profile and brushing history in the app at any time, or contact smilesproutoralcare@gmail.com. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

6. Third-Party Services

SmileSprout uses these Google services to operate. Each processes data under its own privacy terms:

  • Firebase Authentication for sign-in (email and password, or Google). (Firebase Privacy)
  • Cloud Firestore to store synced records. (Google Cloud Privacy)
  • Firebase Storage to store your optional profile photo.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Google Play services for sign-in and system functions.
  • Google ML Kit Translation to translate dental tips on your device. It may download language files; your text is not sent to us.
  • Google Maps is opened only when you tap the dentist search button and is governed by Google's own policy.

7. Android Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show reminders and alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: ring brushing alarms at the exact time you set.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: show the brushing alarm on screen when your phone is locked.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: play the alarm sound and brushing music while the timer runs.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • VIBRATE: vibration during alarms and brushing.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: sync, notifications and loading content.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Choosing a profile photo uses Android's photo picker, which shares only the image you select.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We keep your data only while your account exists. You can delete it at any time:

Pathway A: In the App

  1. Open SmileSprout and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap the red Delete account button.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first, then repeat the steps.

Deleting removes your cloud records (profiles, logs, appointments, notification token and profile photo), your sign-in account, and the data stored on that device. Google may keep residual backup copies for a limited period under its own policies.

Pathway B: By Email

If you no longer have the app, email smilesproutoralcare@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject Data Deletion Request - SmileSprout. We will verify the request and delete your data within 7 business days, then confirm by email.

Data kept only on your phone (guest mode) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

9. Security

  • Encryption in transit: data sent between the app and Google servers uses TLS.
  • Access rules: Firestore and Storage security rules limit each account's data to its own signed-in user.
  • Passwords: passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by us.
  • No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
