Overview & Scope This Privacy Policy applies to the SmileSprout Android application (Package Name: com.aistudio.smilesprout.kqxmpz), developed by Dr. Arnab Santra ("we", "us", or "our"). SmileSprout is a comprehensive family dental care companion offering: • Children Oral Care: Interactive 2-minute brushing timer with musical rhythm pacing, child teething timelines, badging trackers, and pediatric clinical guides; • Pregnancy Oral Hygiene Care: Trimester-specific dental hygiene protocols, gum sensitivity trackers, morning sickness rinse checklists, and prenatal dietary logs; • Routine Reminders: High-priority notifications and exact alarms for morning and nighttime brushing routines and dental clinic visits; • Evidence-Based Education: Clinically validated oral health guidance aligned with recommendations from the American Dental Association (ADA), American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), and World Health Organization (WHO). 2. Information We Collect We believe in data minimization. SmileSprout collects only the information necessary to provide and sync your family oral health tools: A. Information Provided Directly by the User • Account Credentials: When you choose to sync your data across devices, we collect your email address and profile name via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication). You may also use the application in local guest mode without an account. • Parent & Child Profiles: Child's first name or nickname, age or date of birth, and optional avatar choices. These are entered strictly by the parent or legal guardian to calculate age-tailored brushing durations and dental eruption milestones. • Pregnancy Oral Hygiene Records: Gestational week, self-reported dental symptoms (e.g., gum bleeding, pregnancy gingivitis notes), and oral hygiene checklist entries. • Brushing Routine Data: Timestamps of completed 2-minute morning and evening brushing sessions to calculate habit consistency and streak badges. • App Preferences: Selected language (English, বাংলা, हिंदी), reminder sound choices, notification times, and UI preferences. B. Automatically Collected Technical Information • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used strictly to deliver scheduled brushing reminders and clinical oral health tips. • Crash & Performance Diagnostics: Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic data to detect stability issues and optimize application performance. 3. How We Use Information We process your data strictly to deliver core app capabilities: • Habit Tracking & Reminders: To schedule accurate daily brushing reminders and provide countdown timer feedback. • Cloud Synchronization: To back up and restore your family's brushing milestones and pregnancy checklists securely across your Android devices via Google Cloud Firestore. • Clinical Guidance: To display age-appropriate and trimester-appropriate dental education content. • Account Management & Security: To authenticate your identity and safeguard your data against unauthorized access. Commitments to User Trust: • We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal or health information to data brokers or advertising exchanges. • We do not use child profiles or health logs for behavioral targeting or commercial profiling. • We do not access device contacts, microphone, precise GPS location, or photo galleries. 4. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy Compliance Because SmileSprout includes pediatric dental tools for families, we strictly adhere to the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and European GDPR-K regulations: • Parent-Directed Application: SmileSprout is designed and marketed to parents, expectant mothers, and legal guardians. The interactive brushing timer is designed for parents to guide their children during daily hygiene routines. • No Direct Data Solicitation from Children: The app never solicits personal information directly from children under 13. All child profile information (name and age) is input and managed solely by the verified parent or guardian. • Family-Safe Advertising: Any advertisements served within the app conform strictly to Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK requirements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized with child-directed treatment flags (tagForChildDirectedTreatment), ensuring that behavioral tracking and personalized ads are disabled. Parental Rights: Parents and guardians have the right to inspect, update, or completely delete any child profile and brushing log associated with their account at any time through the in-app settings or by contacting smilesproutoralcare@gmail.com. 5. Android Device Permissions In accordance with Google Play's Least Privilege Permission guidelines, SmileSprout requests only permissions strictly necessary for user-facing features: • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures morning and nighttime 2-minute brushing alarms ring reliably at your scheduled time, even while the device is in idle battery optimization (Doze) mode. • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Allows the app to display routine reminders, timer countdowns, and clinical oral hygiene alerts in the notification tray. • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required on Android 14+ to play audio instructions and timer musical pacing continuously while the 2-minute brushing routine is in progress. • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Automatically restores your active morning and nighttime alarms after your phone is rebooted. • VIBRATE: Provides tactile feedback at each 30-second brushing quadrant transition. • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables encrypted cloud synchronization with Firestore and allows loading educational clinical articles. 8. Data Retention & Account Deletion In compliance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion Policy, SmileSprout provides clear pathways for users to permanently delete their account and associated data: Pathway A: In-App Account & Data Deletion Users can permanently delete their account and all associated dental charts directly within the mobile application: 1 Open the SmileSprout app and tap the Profile / Settings tab; 2 Scroll to the Account Management section; 3 Tap Delete Account & All Data and confirm your choice. Upon confirmation, your Firebase Authentication identity and all Firestore documents (child profiles, brushing records, pregnancy checklists, and streaks) are permanently deleted from our servers immediately. Pathway B: Web & Email Data Deletion Request (Remote URL) If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion remotely without reinstalling, send an email to smilesproutoralcare@gmail.com with: • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - SmileSprout • Content: Your registered account email address. All cloud records associated with your account will be permanently purged within 7 business days, and a confirmation email will be sent to you. 9. Security Measures We apply robust administrative and technical security measures to protect your data: • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All network traffic between your device and Google Cloud servers is encrypted using modern TLS cryptographic protocols. • Granular Authorization Rules: Firebase Firestore security rules enforce zero-trust access control, ensuring that only authenticated parents can access their own family dental records. • No Plaintext Passwords: Credentials are handled securely via Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication; no passwords are ever stored on our servers in plaintext. 10.
