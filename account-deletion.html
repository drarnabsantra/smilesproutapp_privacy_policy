Account & Data Deletion Request

SmileSprout puts you in full control of your family's personal health information. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud records at any time.

App: SmileSprout Developer: smilesprout Support: Arnab Santra
Pathway 1: Instant In-App Deletion

Delete Account Directly Within the App

If you have the SmileSprout app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open SmileSprout and tap the Profile / Settings tab at the bottom right.
  2. Scroll down to the bottom of the profile screen.
  3. Tap the red Delete account button.
  4. In the Delete Account? dialog, tap Delete. If you signed in a while ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first; do that, then repeat these steps.

Result: Your account, child dental milestones, brushing habit logs, pregnancy checklists, and cloud database documents are permanently deleted, together with your optional profile photo.

Pathway 2: Remote Web & Email Request

Request Deletion Without Installing the App

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion remotely, you can send a deletion request to our Data Safety Officer:

Or send an email directly to smilesproutoralcare@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request - SmileSprout and your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, the following categories of data are permanently deleted from our servers (Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication):

Data Category Specific Information Purged Status
Account Credentials Google Account UID, email address, profile display name, and auth tokens. Permanently Deleted
Child Profiles Child nickname, age / date of birth, tooth eruption milestone records, and assigned avatar. Permanently Deleted
Brushing Habits Morning and evening 2-minute brushing session timestamps, consistency streaks, and badge rewards. Permanently Deleted
Pregnancy Care Records Gestational week, reported symptoms (e.g. pregnancy gingivitis notes), and prenatal dietary logs. Permanently Deleted
Clinic & Appointments Dental clinic visits, appointment schedules, and reminders. Permanently Deleted
Notification Tokens Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push tokens. Permanently Deleted

Data Retention Period & Requirements

Retention: SmileSprout does not keep your personal or health information after your account is deleted. No personal information is shared or sold. Google may keep residual backup copies for a limited period under its own policies.
  • In-App Deletion: Real-time and instantaneous. Local databases are wiped and Cloud Firestore user collections are deleted immediately.
  • Remote Email Requests: Processed and verified within 7 business days. A confirmation email will be sent to your address once all cloud records have been permanently expunged.
